## 🏷️ Project Title
**Enterprise E-Commerce Sales Performance Dashboard & Behavioral Diagnostics Architecture**

## 📝 Detailed Project Overview
This final mastery phase translates our entire multi-project transactional analytics pipeline into a unified, boardroom-ready dashboard layout using Power BI Desktop. The visualization layer maps exactly **1,200 commercial records across 7 core product categories** to deliver interactive business intelligence, eliminate calculation load, and strictly enforce the data-ink optimization principles of Edward Tufte.

---

### 🎨 Interface Presentation Optimization & Tufte Standards

* **Zero-Baseline Axis Integrity:** All numerical value fields and categorical axes scale strictly from \$0 to absolute calculation limits. This layout configuration entirely eliminates the Tufte Lie Factor caused by truncated charts.
* **Maximized Data-Ink Ratio:** Erased all distracting vertical and horizontal gridlines, bounding frames, and background junk to focus executive visual paths purely on running metrics.
* **Direct Annotation Integration:** Bypassed secondary legend blocks in favor of direct on-chart data labels to minimize short-term memory search friction for corporate reviewers.

---

### 📖 The Strategic Business Storyboard (Situation-Complication-Resolution)

#### 1. The Situation (Current Macro Baseline)
The analytical interface establishes a high-impact summary zone at the top-left hotspot boundary (The 5-Second Executive Scan Rule), displaying a global gross business volume of **\$1.26 Million transacted velocity across 1.2K unique operations**. The retail pipeline maintains a stable operational core centered around our statistical data variables.

#### 2. The Complication (System Vulnerability Risks)
* **Outlier Skewness Distortion:** A massive revenue spread pulls the mean significantly higher than the median, skewed heavily by a distinct tier of hyper-value positive B2B corporate outlier transactions.
* **Product Capital Concentration:** Gross category breakdowns verify that capital accumulation is disproportionately concentrated in two leading assets: **`Chair` (\$196K)** and **`Printer` (\$196K)**, while lagging categories pull down velocity.
* **Temporal Decelerations:** The macro line chart tracks a trailing contraction pipeline from **2023 (\$0.55M)** down to **2024 (\$0.48M)** and a bottom line layout in **2025 (\$0.23M)**.

#### 3. The Resolution (Boardroom Advisory Recommendations)
* **B2B Structural Segregation:** Isolate high-value positive outlier transaction workflows into an independent corporate account track to prevent standard retail distribution distortions.
* **Promotional Campaign Consolidation:** Reallocate marketing budget lines strictly into high-gross yield campaigns calculated in SQL loops: **`FREESHIP` (\$335.0K gross yield)** and **`SAVE10` (\$304.8K gross yield)**.
