### 🎨 Interface Presentation Optimization & Tufte Standards

* **Zero-Baseline Axis Integrity:** All numerical value fields and categorical axes scale strictly from \$0 to absolute calculation limits [1.8]. This layout configuration entirely eliminates the Tufte Lie Factor caused by truncated charts [1.8].

* **Direct Annotation Integration:** Bypassed secondary legend blocks in favor of direct on-chart data labels to minimize short-term memory search friction for corporate reviewers [1.8].

### 📖 The Strategic Business Storyboard (Situation-Complication-Resolution)

#### 1. The Situation (Current Macro Baseline)
The analytical interface establishes a high-impact summary zone at the top-left hotspot boundary (The 5-Second Executive Scan Rule), displaying a global gross business volume of **\$1.26 Million transacted velocity across 1.2K unique operations** [1.8]. The retail pipeline maintains a stable operational core centered around our statistical data variables [1.8].

#### 2. The Complication (System Vulnerability Risks)
* **Outlier Skewness Distortion:** A massive revenue spread pulls the mean significantly higher than the median, skewed heavily by a distinct tier of hyper-value positive B2B corporate outlier transactions [1.8].
* **Product Capital Concentration:** Gross category breakdowns verify that capital accumulation is disproportionately concentrated in two leading assets: **`Chair` (\$196K)** and **`Printer` (\$196K)**, while lagging categories pull down velocity [1.8].
* **Temporal Decelerations:** The macro line chart tracks a trailing contraction pipeline from **2023 (\$0.55M)** down to **2024 (\$0.48M)** and a bottom line layout in **2025 (\$0.23M)** [1.8].

#### 3. The Resolution (Boardroom Advisory Recommendations)
* **B2B Structural Segregation:** Isolate high-value positive outlier transaction workflows into an independent corporate account track to prevent standard retail distribution distortions [1.8].
* **Promotional Campaign Consolidation:** Reallocate marketing budget lines strictly into high-gross yield campaigns calculated in SQL loops: **`FREESHIP` (\$335.0K gross yield)** and **`SAVE10` (\$304.8K gross yield)** [1.8].
